# The concept of flow

@trace spec:research-records

## Claim

The originating author's own summary of flow theory: the conditions under
which people become absorbed in an activity, and the phenomenology of that
state (merging of action and awareness, loss of self-consciousness, distorted
sense of time, the activity as its own reward).

## Evidence

Decades of experience-sampling studies across work, sport, play and learning,
summarised by the theory's author with a co-author. The challenge/skill
balance is the most replicated finding; the exact shape of the "flow channel"
and the number of distinct states around it have been revised over time, so
cite the balance, not a specific diagram.

## How it applies

- Pace challenge to the user's demonstrated skill, not to a fixed schedule.
- Make the next goal visible and give feedback the moment an action lands.
- Anxiety and boredom are both exit signals; instrument for them separately.
