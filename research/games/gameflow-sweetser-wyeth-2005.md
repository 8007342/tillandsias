# GameFlow: eight elements of player enjoyment

@trace spec:research-records

## Claim

Sweetser and Wyeth integrated the game-usability and user-experience
heuristics of their time into one model structured on Csikszentmihalyi's flow:
eight elements, each with concrete criteria, usable as an evaluation checklist.

## Evidence

The model was built by literature synthesis and validated by expert review of
two real-time strategy games, where it distinguished a high-rated game from a
low-rated one on the same criteria. It is a heuristic framework, not a
controlled experiment; its value is as a shared vocabulary and checklist. It
has been widely reused and extended since (including by the same authors).

## How it applies

- When a game feels flat, walk the eight elements before adding features:
  is the goal clear, is feedback immediate, does challenge track skill?
- Difficulty must scale with the player's growing skill; a fixed curve
  violates the challenge/skill element.
- Every element has criteria that can be turned into playtest questions.
