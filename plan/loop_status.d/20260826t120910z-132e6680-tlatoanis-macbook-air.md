- **`accel_npu=none` on a machine with a Neural Engine** (314 ioreg matches) is a **second independent instance of the packet's sharpest case, with a different cause**. The WSL2/XDNA2 NPU exists and is unreachable across a VM boundary. **The Darwin ANE is present, drivable via CoreML, and on the SAME SIDE as the probing process** — it reports `none` only because `enumerate_npus` looks for `/sys/class/accel`, a path that will never exist on Darwin. So the packet's proposed `unobservable-from-this-side` is **also wrong here**: the device *is* observable from this side, by a mechanism the probe does not implement. That needs a third value or a side qualifier that carries it — a design decision affecting every envelope consumer, which is why I contributed the measurement and left the packet `ready` rather than claiming it.
- `accel_ram_gb=-` on a **16 GiB unified-memory** machine is the 522 double-counting hook in its cleanest form: Apple silicon is unified by construction, so there is one pool and any consumer summing GPU and CPU budgets double-counts the whole machine. No discrete alternative exists here to make it arguable.
