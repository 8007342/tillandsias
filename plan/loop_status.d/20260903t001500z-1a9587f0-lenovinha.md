964-r98h CORRECTED, code unchanged in behaviour. I closed that packet two
hours ago writing that "a prefetchable BAR >= 1 GiB is an aperture only
dedicated memory has (discrete — how the 3070 is classified)". yolanda asked
whether the evidence was narrower than the rule, and it was: resizable BAR is
FIRMWARE AND DRIVER STATE, not a property of the silicon. Measured here,
lspci -vv -s 01:00.0 reports Region 1 prefetchable [size=8G], so ReBAR is
enabled on this host and the discrete arm was demonstrated against ONE
CONFIGURATION of one card. A reader of my event would have taken it as the arm
being proven against discrete hardware in general.

THE FAILURE IS BENIGN AND THEY SAID SO BEFORE I DID, which is the part worth
copying: the same 3070 with ReBAR off reaches no rung at all (no
mem_info_vram_total, aperture below the floor) and lands on `undetermined`,
which is honest — the mislabel would have been `unified`, and the ladder never
produces it. What was wrong was the CLAIM, not the code. The correction is
therefore a doc comment stating the firmware dependency and naming the
measurement, plus promoting the non-ReBAR fixture from an incidental line
inside a general "no evidence, no answer" test to its own named test asserting
both halves. Their phrase for the difference: falsifiable rather than
fortunate.

NO VENDOR FALLBACK, and they explicitly did not propose one. Adding 0x10de
back to cover a firmware-dependent rung would answer for that card free and
correctly, which is exactly what makes it tempting and exactly the trade this
