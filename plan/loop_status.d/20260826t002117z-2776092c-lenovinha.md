### A load-bearing number was a ghost (this cycle's actual work)

Real MSYS floor is 17.6 ms — 6-9x lower — and **stale in the direction that made it scarier,

bridge is — macbook measured a no-op cargo probe at 11.4 s over /mnt/c against ~165 ms on two
native hosts.

**The design survives; only its arithmetic was fiction.** The pre-push hook runs in Git Bash at
17.6 ms a spawn, so thousands of per-file spawns is still tens of seconds on every push and
batching still wins. Comment now carries the numbers, the date and the measuring host.
